Put your LCE skin packs here, one .pck per pack.

These are the files the 7th-gen consoles (PS3 / Xbox 360 / Vita) shipped their
DLC skins in. Both byte orders are read and nothing has to be renamed -- the
pack names you see in the game come from inside the files themselves.

    PSP/GAME/MCPSP/data/skinpacks/Skins1.pck
    PSP/GAME/MCPSP/data/skinpacks/SkinPack5.pck
    ...

No packs ship with this port: they are Mojang/4J content and not mine to hand
out. Supply your own.

READ:      .pck skin packs, 64x32 and 64x64 sheets, capes, the extra BOX parts
           that make the mob-shaped skins, and the ANIM bits.
NOT READ:  Bedrock/Java .mcpack or .zip, loose .png files, 128x128 (HD) sheets
           -- a pack whose skins are all HD does not show up -- and Xbox 360 DLC
           downloads, which are containers with a .pck inside and have to be
           unwrapped first.

Making your own: PCK Studio (pckstudio.xyz) opens, edits and builds .pck files.

One skin and no pack at all: replace data/images/skins/skin.png with your own
64x32 or 64x64 PNG.
